About Open Stage Island

Open Stage Island is an evolving music community set in a beautiful natural environment where you bring the magic! This magical realism venue has grown over the past years to become a vibrant social hub.

Join the party, attend DJ school, and dance your avatar's bottom off! Discover freebies and interactive experiences. The open air stage is available 24/7 to all electronic music artists to get live audience feedback. DJs and producers regularly show their skills.
